🏥 In-House Nurse Care App — Proposal & Features
🎯 Purpose of the App
A digital platform that connects patients with certified in-house nurses, allowing real-time booking, monitoring, documentation, and coordination of home-based medical care.
Your app becomes the Uber for nurses, but with hospital-grade workflows.
👥 User Types
1. Patients / Families
They request care, monitor appointments, view nurse reports, and manage billing.
2. In-House Nurses
They manage schedules, perform visits, document vitals, and update patient care logs.
3. Admin / Healthcare Provider
They manage staff, assign cases, oversee compliance, and track performance.
